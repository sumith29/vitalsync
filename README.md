# 🌿 VitalSync – AI-Powered Health Monitoring System

VitalSync is an AI-driven health tracking platform that helps users understand their daily habits and medical reports in a simple, personalized way.  
It uses **dual AI systems** — one for analyzing clinical reports and another for conversational daily check-ins — creating a complete picture of a user’s health.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔹 Daily Health Check-In (AI Chat)
- Conversational chatbot records sleep, meals, mood, and walking activity.
- Asks structured questions for accurate habit tracking.

### 🔹 Clinical Report Upload & AI Summarization
- Upload medical reports in **PDF, JPG, JPEG, or PNG**.
- Gemini Vision AI extracts key values and generates easy-to-read summaries.

### 🔹 Personalized Health Dashboard
- Visual insights of daily logs and weekly health patterns.
- Clean, responsive UI with **light/dark mode** support.

### 🔹 Weekly Trend Analysis
- Shows correlations between habits like sleep, mood, diet, and activity.
- Helps users understand long-term health patterns.

### 🔹 Secure Local Storage
- All data is stored on the user's device for privacy.
- Enables fast, serverless usage.

---

## 🧠 Dual-AI Methodology

### **1️⃣ The Analyst – Clinical Report AI**
- Interprets uploaded lab reports using Gemini Vision.
- Extracts parameters, checks abnormal ranges, and simplifies medical data.
- Ensures clinical information is easy to understand without medical expertise.

### **2️⃣ The Journalist – Lifestyle Tracking AI**
- Handles daily interactive chat to log habits and emotional state.
- Captures sleep, meal patterns, walking levels, and mood.
- Maintains consistent daily entries in a natural, user-friendly way.
